    You need 4 female-to-female wires (10 cm) from your packs. Wire color doesn't matter electrically. These colors just make the guide easy to follow.

    How accurate is this model?

    Matches your hardware
    • Pin order and pin names on both boards, copied from your photos.
    • Pin spacing of 2.54 mm, and the 25.4 mm gap between the ESP32's two pin rows.
    • Board and sensor sizes, within about 1 mm.
    • Positions of the USB-C port, EN and BOOT buttons, and the PWR and D2 LEDs.
    Simplified
    • Tiny parts (resistors, capacitors) are plain blocks.
    • Board text is printed sideways on the real boards; here it is turned to read easily.
    • Wires are drawn neatly. Real 10 cm wires loop and drape.
    • The ESP32's pins point down, so the wire plugs go on from underneath. On your desk the board will rest on those plugs. The HC-SR04 lies on its back with its "eyes" facing up.
