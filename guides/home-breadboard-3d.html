    What this needs (not owned yet)

    • Breadboard (half-size or 830-point; only columns 1–30 are used).
    • NPN transistor S8050 (TO-92) and a 1 kΩ resistor (brown-black-red-gold).
    • 5 male-to-female jumpers (ESP32 pin → breadboard hole) and 1 male-to-male jumper.
    • The LED/buzzer board has solder pads, not pins: 4 wires must be soldered to INPUT, GND, +5V and BB- (soldering iron).

    The CC1101 radio is not on the breadboard: it stays wired straight to the ESP32's right-hand row with its 8 female-to-female wires, as in the CC1101 guide.

    How accurate is this model?

    • Exact: 2.54 mm hole spacing, rows a–e / f–j, the centre channel, which holes are joined inside, every hole used, S8050 pin order (E-B-C, flat side toward you), ESP32 pin names and order.
    • Simplified: part sizes are approximate; the power rails' hole groups and +/− order vary between brands (follow the blue line); the ESP32's pins point down, so its wires plug on from underneath; wires are drawn neatly.
