    What to buy (Amazon, checked 2026-11-14)

    • REXQualis Electronics Component Fun Kit, $14.98: 830-hole breadboard, PN2222 NPN transistors, 1 kΩ resistors (×10), 20 male-female + 65 male-male jumpers.
    • BOJACK breadboards, 2× 830 + 2× 400, $9.99: the second box needs its own breadboard.
    • ELEGOO 120 Dupont wires, $6.98: 40 each of male-female, male-male, female-female (20 cm).

    This build uses 12 male-female, 4 male-male and 14 female-female wires. The LED/buzzer board has solder pads, so its 4 wires need a soldering iron (not in these kits).

    How accurate is this model?

    • Exact: 2.54 mm hole spacing, rows a–e / f–j, which holes are joined inside, every hole used, ESP32 and CC1101 pin names and order, the HC-SR04 and RCWL-0516 pin order (from your photos), transistor leg order E-B-C (S8050 and PN2222, flat face toward you).
    • Simplified: part shapes are approximate blocks; the ESP32's pins point down, so its wires plug on from underneath; rails' hole groups and +/− order vary by brand (follow the red and blue lines); every rail hole used here is in columns 1–29, so rails split in the middle of an 830 board don't matter.
