Project 3 · Solar GPS + LoRa tracker

How the tracker connects

A tiny XIAO ESP32-S3 with a LoRa radio on top and a GPS board underneath. The sun charges a battery through a solar charger. Every few minutes the tracker wakes, gets its position and sends it about 1 km to a second board at home, then sleeps again.

Planned design, not built yet. Sizes and pin positions come from the makers' datasheets and CAD files. Steps marked solder need a soldering iron.

Steps

    XIAO pin plan

    PinUsed for
    D0GPS sleep / wake (L76K, built in)
    D1Battery level, through a 1 MΩ + 1 MΩ divider
    D2GPS reset (L76K V1.1, built in)
    D3Spare: planned switch for sensor power
    D4 / D5I2C sensor bus (SDA / SCL) to the STEMMA QT socket
    D6 / D7GPS serial data (L76K, built in)
    D8–D10LoRa SPI, shared with the radio's connector
    B2BLoRa radio: CS, reset, busy, DIO1, RF switch (GPIO 38–42)

    More sensors share the same two I2C wires (each has its own address), so adding one uses no extra pins.

    Power path

    Panel6 V 2 W (Voltaic P126), 3.5×1.1 mm plug → adapter → 5.5×2.1 mm jack
    ChargerAdafruit bq24074: runs the tracker from the sun first, charges the rest into the battery, stops charging outside 0–50 °C
    BatteryProtected 18650, 2200 mAh: about 5 days with no sun
    USB-C #1Waterproof wall socket → charger's USB-C: power + charging, works together with the panel
    USB-C #2Waterproof wall socket → XIAO's USB-C: programming and serial monitor
    XIAOFed on its BAT pads from the charger's LOAD output (≤ 4.4 V)
    Radio916.0 MHz, +14 dBm max, 1% of the time (Israeli rules)

    How accurate is this model?

    From the makers' files
    • XIAO ESP32-S3 size (21 × 17.8 mm), pin order, BAT pad positions: Seeed DXF and STEP.
    • bq24074 board (38.1 × 33.0 mm), header order, JST and jack positions, mounting holes: Adafruit EAGLE and STEP files.
    • Solar panel 112 × 136 mm with its junction box; 18650 cell 69 × 18 mm; GPS patch 25 × 25 × 8.5 mm.
    Simplified or estimated
    • Board components are blocks. The Wio-SX1262 and L76K boards are drawn as plain boards with their main parts.
    • Stack heights (B2B gap 3.5 mm, socket height 8.5 mm) are estimates. Measure the real parts before the box is printed.
    • The long stacking pins under the GPS board are our plan for plugging extra wires in. They need soldering onto the GPS board.
    • Parts sit where the box draft puts them (cad/tracker_box_draft.FCStd): blue lines = box walls, amber = battery bay, pink = sensor bay. Walls and lid aren't drawn so you can see inside; the solar panel (the lid) is drawn beside the box.
