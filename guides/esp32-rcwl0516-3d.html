Wire the RCWL-0516 radar to your ESP32

Steps

    Connections

    WireESP32 pinRadar pin

    You need 3 female-to-female wires (10 cm) from your packs, and no breadboard, once the radar's header is soldered. Wire color doesn't matter electrically. These colors just make the guide easy to follow. The radar's 3V3 and CDS pins stay empty.

    How accurate is this model?

    Matches your hardware
    • Pin order and pin names on both boards, copied from your photos (radar: 3V3 GND OUT VIN CDS, see photos/rcwl0516-package-label.jpg).
    • Pin spacing of 2.54 mm, and the 25.4 mm gap between the ESP32's two pin rows.
    • ESP32 size within about 1 mm. Radar board 35.9 × 17.3 mm, the commonly published size (not measured on yours). Its layout follows your photo photos/rcwl0516-front-header-unsoldered.jpg: pin holes on one short end, the chip lengthwise beside them, the wavy antenna, transistor and diamond CDS pad on the other half.
    • Positions of the USB-C port, EN and BOOT buttons, and the PWR and D2 LEDs.
    Simplified
    • Tiny parts (resistors, capacitors, the radar chip) are plain blocks, and their positions on the radar are approximate.
    • Board text is printed sideways on the real boards; here it is turned to read easily.
    • Wires are drawn neatly. Real 10 cm wires loop and drape.
    • The ESP32's pins point down, so the wire plugs go on from underneath. On your desk the board will rest on those plugs.
    • The radar is shown after soldering, with the header's long pins pointing down like the ESP32's. Yours came with the header loose. In use it senses in every direction and through plastic and thin walls; keep metal away from its back.
